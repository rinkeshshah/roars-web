WHAT WE DO

Our UI and UX design services in Birmingham

  • 01

    UI & UX Design

    Web and mobile interfaces people move through without thinking. Layout, hierarchy, states and motion, designed to be built rather than admired.

  • 02

    UX Consulting

    Direction before pixels. We set the approach at the outset so revisions stay small and the budget stays where you put it.

  • 03

    UX Research & Strategy

    Interviews, analytics and competitor teardowns. We find out what users actually do before anybody opens a design tool.

  • 04

    Usability Testing

    Structured tests with real users, run before launch. Every issue comes back with a severity, a screen and a fix.

  • 05

    UX Auditing

    A full read of your product against usability heuristics and your own analytics. You get a prioritised list, not a lecture.

  • 06

    Wireframing & Prototyping

    Clickable prototypes in days. We test the flow while it is still cheap to change, then take the winner through to final UI.

HOW WE WORK

Our UI and UX design process

  1. WEEK 1 / DISCOVERY

    Map the mindset

    We follow your users end to end. Every touchpoint, every drop-off, every moment the interface gets in the way.

  2. WEEK 2 / DEFINE

    Wireframe the argument

    Structure settled before anybody argues about colour. UX standards meet your brand, in that order.

  3. WEEK 3 to 4 / DESIGN

    Make it yours

    Visual design, components, states, motion. Built as a system, so the tenth screen costs less than the first.

  4. WEEK 5 / VALIDATE

    Test, then hand over

    Usability testing with real users, fixes applied, then a handover your engineers can build from without guessing.
