Travel and hospitality app development

WHAT IS DIFFERENT HERE

What makes travel software different

  1. 01

    The value is in the disruption

    Search and booking are solved. What decides whether a traveller trusts a product is what it does at the gate when the flight moves.

  2. 02

    Inventory belongs to somebody else

    Availability, price and cancellation rules live in systems you do not control and do not agree with each other. The product has to be honest about that.

  3. 03

    Connectivity is the worst you design for

    Airports, foreign networks, roaming turned off. Anything that only works with a good connection does not work when it matters.

  4. 04

    Time zones are a correctness problem

    Not a formatting one. Most travel bugs that reach a customer are a date that was right in one place and wrong in another.

WHERE THIS GOES WRONG

Where travel builds usually come apart

  • A beautiful search and nothing after booking

    Everything goes into the funnel and nothing into the trip. The traveller’s hardest moments all happen after payment.

  • Treating supplier data as reliable

    Prices and availability get cached optimistically, and the failure shows up as a booking that cannot be honoured.

  • Notifications that arrive too late to help

    A gate change alert that lands after the gate change is worse than none. The timing is the feature.

WHAT WE DO IN THIS SECTOR

What we do for travel and hospitality clients

  • Mobile app developmentThe app a traveller opens at the gate, built for the worst connection rather than the office wifi.
  • Product developmentThe itinerary model underneath, which is where supplier data and time zones become correctness problems.
  • UX designThe disruption flows, which are the ones that decide whether the traveller trusts the product again.
  • Web app developmentThe operations side, where the staff resolving the disruption actually work.
