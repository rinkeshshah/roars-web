WHAT IS DIFFERENT HERE

What makes healthcare software different

  1. 01

    The user is not at their best

    A patient is anxious and a clinician is between appointments. Anything that needs full attention to use correctly will be used incorrectly.

  2. 02

    The rules are not a preference

    Data handling, consent and retention are set externally and change on somebody else’s schedule. They shape the architecture, not the settings screen.

  3. 03

    Integration with systems older than you

    Records live in platforms that were not designed to be integrated with. What is possible is decided by their interfaces, not by yours.

  4. 04

    Wrong is worse than slow

    Most software optimises for speed. Here the cost of a confident wrong answer is high enough that the design has to prefer asking.

WHERE THIS GOES WRONG

Where healthcare builds usually come apart

  • Compliance treated as a final phase

    Built first and reviewed at the end, which is when the data model turns out to be the thing that has to change.

  • Designed for the clinician, given to the patient

    Or the reverse. The two use it in completely different states and a single interface serving both usually serves neither.

  • A portal nobody opens twice

    Access is granted, the login is awkward, and the phone call it was meant to replace keeps happening.
