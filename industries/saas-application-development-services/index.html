WHAT IS DIFFERENT HERE

What makes SaaS different

  1. 01

    Activation decides everything downstream

    Churn, expansion and support load are all mostly set in the first week. Work spent further down the funnel is usually work spent on a symptom.

  2. 02

    Multi-tenancy is an early decision

    How tenants are isolated shapes the schema, the permissions and the cost of every enterprise conversation you will have later. It is hard to revisit.

  3. 03

    Every release reaches everyone at once

    There is no staged rollout to a physical population. Feature flags and a tested rollback are what make shipping frequently survivable.

  4. 04

    The buyer and the user are different people

    One signs, the other decides whether it gets used. A product that only serves the buyer renews once.

WHERE THIS GOES WRONG

Where SaaS builds usually come apart

  • An empty state that explains nothing

    The new account opens on a dashboard with no data in it. The most important screen in the product is the one that gets the least design attention.

  • Features added to close deals

    Each one was reasonable in the room. Together they are a product with no shape, and a roadmap owned by whoever was last on a call.

  • Onboarding treated as a marketing job

    A tour and an email sequence bolted onto a product that has not changed. Activation is a product problem, and tours are what people do instead of fixing it.
