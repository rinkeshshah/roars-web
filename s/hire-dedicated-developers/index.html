THE COST OF WAITING

Four months of hiring is four months of not shipping.

Plus agency fees, the offers that fall through, and the ramp-up after they start. The roadmap does not pause while you recruit. It just gets longer, and the reason it slipped stops being visible to anyone above you.

HOW IT RUNS

People who join your team, not a ticket queue

  1. 01Week 1

    Enquiry and analysis

    An honest read on what you actually need. Sometimes it is two people rather than five, and we will say so before you have signed anything.

  2. 02Weeks 2 to 3

    Wireframe and design

    If the work needs design, it gets design. Handing engineers an undefined brief is how augmentation ends up slower than hiring.

  3. 03Ongoing

    Development and delivery

    The same named people in your stand-ups, working your board, with code reviewed against your standards rather than ours.

SCOPE

What a dedicated team includes, and what it does not

In

  • Named people, full time, on your board and in your stand-ups
  • Code reviewed against your standards
  • Design capacity when the work needs it
  • A minimum three month commitment, both ways
  • Direct contact with the engineers, not through an account manager
  • Notice periods that are the same in both directions

Out

  • Deciding your roadmap, that is your job or a different engagement
  • Paying for someone to sit idle while you decide
  • Swapping people out without telling you
  • Hourly billing against a ticket queue
  • Anyone who has not worked with the rest of the team before
THE SHAPE OF IT

What you are signing up to

Typical duration
Three month minimum, monthly after that, thirty days notice either way.
Who you work with
  • EngineersTwo to six, sized to the work. Named people, and you meet them before you commit.
  • DesignerPart time or full time, only when the work needs one.
  • Delivery leadIncluded, not billed. There so you have one person to talk to when something is wrong.
What we need from you
  • A backlog that is ready enough to start on in week one
  • Someone on your side who can answer a question the same day
  • Access to your repository, environments and standards documentation
How pricing works
Monthly per person, all in. No recruitment fee, no charge for the delivery lead, no markup on tools. [ NEEDS THE REAL BAND: monthly rate per engineer ]

HOW WE WORK

Our Digital Business Transformation Process

  1. Project enquiry & analysis

    When we receive your inquiry for hire dedicated developers, one of our app development consultants connects with you and understands your project requirements in details so that we can provide you the estimated cost and time period to complete your project.

  2. Wire framing & UI/UX

    After understanding your requirement, we screen developers and designers from our company and send their resumes over to you for interview. Once you shortlist and hire them, we start the primary process in which our designers help you gain a vision for your project or customize your existing design.
