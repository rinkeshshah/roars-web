THE COST OF WAITING

A funnel that leaks quietly is the most expensive kind.

Nothing errors, nobody complains, and the number just sits lower than it should. Every month you spend acquiring users into a flow that loses them, you are paying full price for a fraction of the value.

HOW IT RUNS

Find the drop, fix the drop, prove it moved

  1. 01Weeks 1 to 2

    Research

    You get the map of where people actually leave, from your own analytics and from watching real users, with the three places worth fixing ranked by what they cost you.

  2. 02Weeks 3 to 4

    Wireframe

    The reworked flows as wireframes, with the reasoning for each change written next to it. You can argue with the reasoning before anyone designs a pixel.

  3. 03Weeks 5 to 7

    Interface

    Finished screens and the components behind them, handed to your engineers in a form they can build from without asking what a state does.

  4. 04Week 8 onward

    Measure

    The events needed to tell whether the change worked, and a read of the first weeks of data once it is live.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

FlowRow

A fitness app where people signed up in good numbers and stopped opening it somewhere in the first fortnight.

  • Traced the drop to the gap between signing up and the first session that felt worth doing.
  • Rebuilt the opening flow around one session rather than around a profile, a plan and a goal.

SCOPE

What a UX engagement includes, and what it does not

In

  • Analytics review and user interviews
  • A ranked list of where the product loses people
  • Reworked flows as wireframes, with the reasoning
  • Finished interface design and a component set
  • Developer handoff with states and edge cases drawn
  • The event tracking needed to prove it worked

Out

  • Building the design, unless you also engage us to develop
  • Brand identity, logos or a visual language from scratch
  • Marketing site design
  • Ongoing design support on a retainer
  • Redesigning everything, we work on the parts that cost you money
THE SHAPE OF IT

What you are signing up to

Typical duration
Seven to nine weeks, depending on how many flows are in scope.
Who you work with
  • UX leadRuns the research and owns the argument for every change.
  • Interface designerTakes the agreed flows to finished screens and a component set.
  • ResearcherRuns the interviews and the usability sessions, part time across the engagement.
What we need from you
  • Read access to your analytics, on day one rather than week three
  • Introductions to five current users and, if you can, two who left
  • One person who can approve a direction without a committee
How pricing works
Priced per engagement against the flows in scope, agreed before we start. Research and design are not billed separately, because splitting them is how you end up with pretty screens nobody validated. [ NEEDS THE REAL BAND: typical UX engagement range ]
