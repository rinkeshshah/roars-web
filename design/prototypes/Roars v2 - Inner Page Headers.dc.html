Turn 5 · inner page headers
Five header types cover every inner page
All five are the same object as the 4a hero, cut shorter: a grained field band under the white nav bar. Light field carries black ink, dark field carries white. Each frame shows the band plus the first rows of white content so the handoff reads. Badges: 5a 5b 5c 5d 5e.
Where grain goes
Home hero · inner page header bands · CTA band · footer · full-bleed stat or quote bands. Nowhere else — white content sections, cards and tables stay clean, so the grain reads as the brand's one texture rather than a filter over the whole site.
