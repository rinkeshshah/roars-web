4a Grain field one colour, real noise, one word that changes
Built from the reference, translated to black and yellow: a full-bleed grained field with black ink on it, and exactly one moving part in the type — the last word of the headline swaps on a slow cycle, landing whole rather than mid-stroke. The grain boils at 12fps and two blooms drift underneath on 28 and 36 second cycles, so the surface breathes without anything sliding. Footer inverts it: same grain, near-black field, one yellow bloom behind the wordmark.

3a Liquid chrome molten wordmark · pointer drags the metal
The wordmark is rendered as poured chrome with one yellow band running through it, then sliced into 2px rows and displaced — so it never stops flowing. In the footer the same metal has run down to the bottom edge and pooled: the drip amplitude grows toward the floor and the pool reflects the nav.
