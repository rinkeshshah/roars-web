Signature element — hero & footer build-up
The element becomes the background
Each candidate is shown as a scale ladder and then as the hero and the footer of the same page. Nothing decorative is added: the ground is the signature element enlarged, and the same shape reappears at UI size in the rail, the button and the footer rule. If the hero and the footer are built from one shape, the pages between them inherit it for free.
