Turn 8 · one idea, four cuts
The page name at masthead scale, held by one rule
One concept, not four. The homepage gets its scale from a 212px wordmark on yellow; inner pages get theirs from the page name itself — 200 to 300px, Inter 600 at −0.055em, cropped or flush to a gutter, with a single rule as the spine everything else hangs off. Nothing here invents a colour, a typeface or an effect the system does not already have. Two structures × two grounds: 8a 8b 8c 8d.
8a Crop · dark 300px name, bled left, cut by the band edge
The name runs off the left gutter at 300px and the band cuts its baseline, so the word continues past the page. A yellow hairline spine at 500 carries the stat row on its left and the quote sits above it on the right. The scale is the effect — no glow needed.

Turn 7 · inner header, own identity
Four fields that stop borrowing the homepage
The homepage hero is a yellow wash — so an inner header built the same way reads as a smaller homepage. These four each take a different structural idea instead: paper and rules, a black plate, type as the aperture, and a hard vertical split. Same content, same positions. 7a 7b 7c 7d.
7a Paper and rules warm paper, the 1440 grid drawn, one yellow chip
No wash at all. Warm paper ground with the page's own column rules (39 / 503 / 967 / 1401) drawn as hairlines and the grain dialled right down, so the field reads as a measured register rather than a hero. The yellow survives as one object: the count chip.
