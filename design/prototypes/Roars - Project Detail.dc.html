About:

A compliance-first warehouse operations app for India’s largest cold-chain network — pairing field-ready checklists with real-time oversight. From audit mapping to rollout, we built a product that keeps every facility inspection-ready.

Year2026
ClientSnowman Logistics

Goals of the Project:

Our goal was to replace paper audits with a single digital system that supervisors could actually use on the floor — offline-capable, photo-backed, and fast enough to finish a full facility round without slowing operations down.

To standardise inspections across every warehouse, cut reporting lag from days to minutes, and give leadership one live view of compliance health.

{{ g.label }}
Outcome:

Supervisors now complete a facility round in a fraction of the time, with photo evidence attached at every step. Compliance reporting is continuous instead of monthly, and leadership can see which sites need attention the same day.

A field-ready checklist app with offline sync, role-based oversight, and a live compliance dashboard rolled out across the network.
