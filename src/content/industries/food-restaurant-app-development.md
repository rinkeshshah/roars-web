We build the best-in-class solutions that help you serve your diners better.

A restaurant is not one app. It is six moments that have to agree with each
other. We design and build the surfaces each moment needs, and the plumbing
that keeps them in step.

## Discovery

Menus, hours, reviews and location-aware discovery, so the people already three
streets away end up at your table instead of somebody else's.

## Ordering

Modifiers, allergens, combos and upsells that read the same on a phone at the
bus stop and on a tablet at the pass. The hard part is not the menu, it is
keeping one menu true across every surface that shows it.

## Payment

Cards, wallets, net banking, promo codes and split bills, with the failure
states designed rather than left to chance. A declined card at the end of a
good meal is a design problem, not a payments problem.

## Kitchen

Tickets land on the display grouped by station. Prep timers, holds, and an 86
list that updates every other surface at once, so the app stops selling what
the kitchen has run out of.

## Delivery

Runner assignment, waiter handoff, live tracking and the alerts that keep a full
floor from turning into guesswork.

## Why it is built this way

Each of those moments is usually sold as its own product, which is how
restaurants end up with five systems that disagree about the price of a side.
The work that matters is the agreement between them: one source of truth for the
menu, one for availability, one for the order, and interfaces that are honest
about which of the three they are showing.

That is also what makes the difference measurable. When the kitchen display and
the ordering surface share state, the 86 list stops being a shout across a
kitchen and starts being a number you can watch.
