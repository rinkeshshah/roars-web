## What is User Experience?

Customer feel dictates business sales and significantly influences decisions. At the end of the day, customers are what matter and their engagement into a business lead to delightful benefits and serves the target prospects. The feel a customer gains by interacting on primarily a digital platform is referred to as User Experience or UX.

**Simplifying user interaction for a favorable experience!**

Every website or app interaction has a certain goal. The ease of access and engagement which leads a visitor to the goal effortlessly and quickly defines a better User Experience. Thus a seamless online interaction yields a delightful customer experience and best fulfills his/her needs. User Experience helps retain clients and develops a loyalty towards the brand.

Every visitor comes with a certain expectation for a service and it is UX that determines how well he is being served. User experience is proportional to visual identity. While visual design and website aesthetics take the top spot, it is UX design that best complements them to derive the most from each.

A creative UX design ensures that a website layout and content is best suited for the target audience and that the platform feels reliable and is vastly effective.

### The state of UX as per 2021 trends:

The design is a constantly adapting and evolving discipline as customer demands keep changing from time to time. While the future is hard to predict, we tend to offer what is most innovative and has a definite scope of utilization in the times to come. With a key aim to help businesses eliminate flaws and design anomalies, the following hot trends in UX design will help enhance user satisfaction and simplify visitor-system interaction –

**1) Minimalism**

#### To focus on what is important!

Distractions need to be eliminated from the website design to let the user focus on the catchiest of stuff. The key elements of the design need to be presented in a very accessible manner with content being at the center of attention. A well-curated and accessible content works wonders in delivering the key message and when complemented with attractive graphics, makes the visitor best understand the deliverables for a delightful experience

**2) Easy-to-consume layout**

#### User-specific design for effortless access

For an intuitive user experience, a gradually displayed content makes it easy to grab what is being offered, rather than getting caught up by the complex presentation without a clear sense of purpose. This linear user flow helps save time and with a limited number of actions to be performed serves a clear navigation from favorable architecture.

**3) Individually tailored experience**

Personalization is an effective strategy to best involve visitors and get them interested in the relevant products and services.
