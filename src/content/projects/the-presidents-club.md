## Color Therapy

We are elated that Samsung chose us to deliver the app interface for The President’s Club, that will be used by their clients on their phones.

We have designed customer loyalty program app interface to give it an elegant and sophisticated look by using the colors black, gold, and white.

Under each tab, opens up a new page to select further and narrow down to the item the user is searching for. Everything can be found no more than 2 levels down. By choosing their location, users can find an array of all President Club powered services and offerings in their vicinity.
