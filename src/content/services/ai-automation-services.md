We don't sell AI. We ship small, specific systems that remove friction from the
work your team already does, then we measure whether they helped. If they
didn't, we turn them off.

## What we build

**Goal-driven assistants** that can read a ticket, query your CRM, draft a
response, and route it for review. Built for one job, evaluated like software,
not demoed like magic.

**Workflow automation** end to end, across the apps you already pay for.
Trigger from Slack, read from Salesforce, write to Notion, post to Linear, with
an LLM doing the thinking in between.

**Retrieval systems**, private, trained on your contracts, docs, tickets and
codebase. Answers cite their sources. No hallucinated policies. No "based on my
training data" disclaimers.

**Document extraction** that pulls structured data out of PDFs, invoices,
contracts and emails, validates it against your schema, and hands only the edge
cases to a human. The other 80% just happens.

**The plumbing.** Private model routing, prompt management, telemetry and cost
controls, so AI works inside the products and tools your team already lives in.
Unglamorous, and the reason it survives production.

## How an engagement runs

Every Roars engagement starts with the same question: does this actually help
someone? If it doesn't, we tell you before we take the brief. If it does, this
is how we get from idea to in-production.

**Map.** We sit with your team and map the work. Not the org chart, the actual
hour-by-hour. We're looking for friction, repetition, and the moments where a
person is doing what a system should.

**Design.** Data flows, decision points, edge cases, human-in-the-loop gates.
We draw the whole thing before we touch a model. You sign off on a diagram, not
a vibe.

**Pilot.** A working system inside your stack. Real data, real users, real
tests. We write evals before we write prompts. Nothing ships without measurable
proof it's better than the status quo.

**Operate.** We instrument every decision the system makes. You see what it
cost, what it saved, and what it got wrong. We hand off runbooks and train your
team to own it, or we stay on retainer. Your call.

## The two-week audit

Two weeks. We sit with your team, map the work, and find the three highest-ROI
automations and the three to never attempt. You get a build plan and a clear
"no", if that is what the work deserves.
